About This Tool

Uses pdf-lib to split and extract PDF pages entirely inside your browser. You can split a PDF into every individual page, or enter a custom range like 1-3, 5, 8-10 to extract exactly the pages you need. No files are ever uploaded to a server.

How to Split a PDF Online

1. Upload a PDF
Choose a PDF file from your device using the upload area.
2. Choose split mode
Split every page into separate PDFs, or enter a custom range such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?
No. All splitting is done using pdf-lib which runs entirely in your browser. Your documents are never transmitted to any server and remain completely private on your device.
Can I split specific pages?
Yes. You can enter custom page ranges to extract exactly the pages you need. For example, enter "1-3, 5, 7-9" to extract those specific pages into a new PDF.
Can I split encrypted PDFs?
Yes. Encryption restrictions are handled during the splitting process, allowing you to extract pages from protected PDF documents into new unencrypted files.
